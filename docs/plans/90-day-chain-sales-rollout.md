# 90-Day Chain Sales Rollout Plan

Date: 2026-02-21
Objective: Sign and retain multi-branch dialysis groups in Malaysia

## 1) Analytics Context

Source: GA4 property `469818591` on 2026-02-21
Baseline window: 2026-01-22 to 2026-02-20

- Users: 4,937
- Sessions: 5,972
- Organic Search share: 79.9%
- Mobile share: 59.8%
- `form_start` users: 4 (tracking gap for actual submitted leads)

What this means:
- Demand exists.
- Main bottleneck is lead capture measurement and conversion flow reliability.

## 2) 90-Day Targets

- Sign 3 paying chain clients.
- Launch 60+ branch pages under paid plans.
- Deliver 150 qualified leads.
- Achieve at least 1 renewal discussion by day 75.

## 3) ICP and Account List

Ideal customer profile:
- Has 5 or more dialysis branches.
- Has central HQ or marketing/operations lead.
- Currently spends on offline awareness but lacks measurable digital leads.

Account list build:
- Target list size: 30 chains.
- Priority tier A: 10 largest chain operators.
- Priority tier B: next 20 growing operators.

## 4) Offer Positioning

Core pitch:
- "We help your chain fill chairs faster with location-based qualified leads and branch-level response tracking."

Proof points to show:
- Malaysia-focused patient search intent.
- Branch-level pages with local SEO value.
- Lead replacement guarantee reduces acquisition risk.

## 5) Weekly Action Plan

### Week 1-2: Build Sales Infrastructure
- Finalize package sheet and contract template.
- Build 2 live demos:
- Chain profile demo
- Branch page demo
- Prepare outreach script and objection handling sheet.
- Complete analytics foundation before paid lead sales:
- Add `lead_form_submit` and set as key event.
- Add `lead_whatsapp_click` and `lead_phone_click`.
- Create weekly dashboard for users, sessions, lead events, and session-to-lead rate.

### Week 3-4: Outbound Prospecting
- Contact 30 target chains.
- Goal: 10 discovery calls.
- Send custom mockup to each discovery call account.

### Week 5-6: Pilot Closing
- Close 1 pilot client with discounted 30-day pilot.
- Launch their pages and lead workflow in 7 days.
- Start weekly reporting with branch metrics.
- Pilot success floor:
- Session-to-lead rate >= 0.8%
- Response time median < 15 minutes

### Week 7-8: Case Study Build
- Collect pilot results:
- Leads delivered
- Response time improvement
- Booking conversion
- Turn results into 1-page case study.

### Week 9-10: Scale Closing
- Use pilot case study to close 2 more chains.
- Focus on operations/HQ buyers, not only branch managers.
- Enforce package floor pricing to avoid margin erosion.

### Week 11-12: Retention and Expansion
- Run QBR-style review for all signed accounts.
- Propose expansion:
- More featured branches
- More states coverage
- Higher lead cap
- Prepare renewal offers before day 90.

## 6) Sales Process and Stage Exit Criteria

### Stage 1: Discovery
- Exit when all confirmed:
- Branch count
- Target geographies
- Current patient acquisition channels
- Decision maker and budget owner

### Stage 2: Proposal
- Exit when proposal includes:
- Package + pricing
- Lead definition
- SLA and replacement policy

### Stage 3: Pilot/Contract
- Exit when signed order form and branch contact list received.

### Stage 4: Launch
- Exit when all branch pages are live, test leads route correctly, and lead key events are recording in GA4.

### Stage 5: Optimize/Renew
- Exit when monthly report is reviewed and next-term plan is agreed.

## 7) KPIs by Function

Sales KPIs:
- Outreach-to-call rate target: 30%.
- Call-to-proposal rate target: 60%.
- Proposal-to-close rate target: 30%.

Operations KPIs:
- Lead routing accuracy target: 95%+.
- Median first response target: under 15 minutes.
- Invalid lead replacement turnaround: under 3 business days.
- Session-to-lead rate target: >= 0.8% first month, >= 1.2% by month 3.

## 8) Risk Register and Mitigation

Risk: Low branch response speed
- Mitigation: SLA fallback route to backup branch and weekly compliance review.

Risk: Client disputes lead quality
- Mitigation: Strict qualification checklist + reason-coded audit trail.

Risk: Long enterprise decision cycle
- Mitigation: Offer low-friction 30-day pilot with clear conversion milestones.

Risk: Traffic is high but lead events are under-tracked
- Mitigation: Do not sell outcome guarantees until lead event instrumentation is verified.
