# dialisis.my

Malaysian dialysis center directory.

## Design Context

### Users
Family members searching for dialysis care options for loved ones. They're navigating a stressful healthcare decision and need quick access to reliable information about nearby centers, services offered, and contact details.

### Brand Personality
**Trustworthy, calm, professional.** The interface should feel like a knowledgeable friend in healthcare - competent without being cold, reassuring without being patronizing.

### Emotional Goals
Build **confidence and trust** that users are finding the right care. Every interaction should reduce anxiety and increase certainty in their decision-making.

### Design Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness** - Healthcare decisions are stressful. Make information scannable and actions obvious. No cognitive overhead.

2. **Trust through consistency** - Use the established green (#2edb84) and navy (#042c53) palette consistently. Maintain visual rhythm across components.

3. **Accessible by default** - WCAG AA compliance minimum. Consider elderly family members: adequate text sizes, high contrast, clear touch targets.

4. **Mobile-first, family-second** - Primary searches happen on phones, often in hospital waiting rooms. Desktop serves deeper research.

5. **Calm urgency** - Users need to act but shouldn't feel panicked. Efficient layouts, but warm not clinical.

### Visual Guidelines

- **Colors**: Primary green (148.49 73.06% 51.96%), navy foreground (206.75 97.65% 16.67%)
- **Typography**: Geist Sans (clean, modern, readable)
- **Spacing**: Generous whitespace, cards with subtle shadows
- **Animations**: Fast (0.2-0.3s), ease-out, reduce motion for accessibility
- **Components**: shadcn/ui base, Radix primitives
